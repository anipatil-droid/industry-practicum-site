About the course

BMGT407 - Information Systems Projects

BMGT407 is the capstone course for Information Systems majors, where students apply their knowledge to develop real-world IS solutions for business clients.

Through a complete information-systems development process, students design and implement functional prototypes that address practical business challenges. The course prepares students for careers in technology, consulting, and digital transformation.
